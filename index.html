Who We Are

A decade of engineering the world's cleaner grid

Founded in 2014 in Zurich, Lumina Dynamics began as a small team of energy engineers with one conviction: sustainability and reliability shouldn't be a trade-off. Today we design, deploy, and maintain smart energy infrastructure for utilities, municipalities, and industry leaders.

With regional hubs in Dubai, Singapore, and Austin, our teams combine local grid expertise with a shared engineering standard — so every Lumina deployment performs the same, wherever it stands.
